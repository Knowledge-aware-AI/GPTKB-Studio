Accessibility

This accessibility statement applies to https://gptkbstudio.scads.ai. This website is run by ScaDS.AI Dresden/Leipzig. ScaDS.AI Dresden/Leipzig is committed to making its websites and mobile applications accessible, in accordance with the “Website Accessibility Act” BfWebG (Barrierefreie-Websites-Gesetz) and the “Accessible IT Ordinance” BITV 2.0 (Barrierefreie-Informationstechnik-Verordnung), implementing EU Directive 2016/2102.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on 03.11.2026 by means of self-assessment. The basis for issuing this accessibility statement is self-assessment of the website's compatibility with the requirements of the BfWebG in accordance with BITV 2.0 carried out by TU Dresden University of Technology.

Compliance status

Due to the following non-compliances, this website is partially compliant with the requirements of the BfWebG in conjunction with BITV 2.0.

  • Accessibility by keyboard is not fully available for the following elements/content:
    • The interactive knowledge-graph view in the run reports
    • Resizing the sidebar and the panels of the dashboard
