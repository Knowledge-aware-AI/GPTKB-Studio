Data Protection Declaration

TU Dresden processes personal data within the framework of the use of the publicly accessible website (gptkbstudio.scads.ai) only as far as this is necessary for the provision of the service. The website uses no cookies, and no tracking is used to record or analyze user movements and the surfing behavior of users of our site.

Server logs

When you open the website, the web server records the IP address of your device, the time and the requested address, in order to operate the service and to protect it against misuse. These records are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Settings in your browser

The website keeps your display settings (open run tabs, color theme, layout) in the local storage of your browser. They are not transmitted to the server, and you can delete them at any time in your browser.

Runs and their inputs

The topics, seed entities, prompts and settings you enter, and the knowledge bases built from them, are stored on the server and are visible to all visitors of the website in the Run Explorer. Please do not enter personal data in them.

API keys that you enter are used only for the requests of your run to the endpoint you entered them for. They are kept in memory only while the run is going and are never stored.

Language-model services

To build a knowledge base, the topic, the seed entity, the prompt and the facts collected so far are sent to the language-model service you choose. By default this is the inference service of ScaDS.AI at TU Dresden. If you choose another service (for example OpenAI, OpenRouter or an endpoint of your own), its terms of use and data protection declaration apply to these requests.

Legal basis

The legal basis for this is Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR.

You have the right to obtain information from TU Dresden about the data stored about your person and/or to have incorrectly stored data corrected.

You have the right to erasure or restriction of the processing and/or a right to object to the processing.
